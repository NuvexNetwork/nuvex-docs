# AI oracle

Not implemented. `ai/` in the nuvex-services repository is a directory and a README. There is no model, no runtime, and no claim that an output is verified.

A future job may commit to a model digest and an output. A commitment is not a proof of correct inference. zkML is a later research milestone and is not scheduled until proof time, size, and cost are measured.
