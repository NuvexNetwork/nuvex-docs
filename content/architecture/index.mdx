# Architecture

Three programs:

- oracle core, for requests, fees, and callbacks
- oracle registry, for nodes, stake, heartbeats, and slash
- verification, for proofs and later challenges

Decision records live in `docs/architecture/adr/`. They are the source of truth:

- `0001-core-architecture.md` — program split and the transition graph
- `0002-account-model.md` — PDA seeds, authority, and rent
- `0003-node-selection.md` — eligibility predicate for VRF fulfillment; no weighted draw
- `0004-verification-model.md` — `solana-ecvrf` 0.0.1, with no audit found
- `0005-fee-model.md` — fee roles; basis points are not chosen
- `0006-callback-model.md` — callback with zero accounts
- `0007-upgrade-model.md` — multisig upgrades; nothing is deployed
