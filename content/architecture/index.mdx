# Architecture

Three programs:

- oracle core, for requests, fees, and callbacks
- oracle registry, for nodes, stake, heartbeats, and slash
- verification, for proofs and later challenges

Decision records live in this repository under `architecture/adr/`. They are the source of truth:

- [ADR 0001](https://github.com/NuvexNetwork/nuvex-docs/blob/main/architecture/adr/0001-core-architecture.md) — program split and the transition graph
- [ADR 0002](https://github.com/NuvexNetwork/nuvex-docs/blob/main/architecture/adr/0002-account-model.md) — PDA seeds, authority, and rent
- [ADR 0003](https://github.com/NuvexNetwork/nuvex-docs/blob/main/architecture/adr/0003-node-selection.md) — eligibility predicate for VRF fulfillment; no weighted draw
- [ADR 0004](https://github.com/NuvexNetwork/nuvex-docs/blob/main/architecture/adr/0004-verification-model.md) — `solana-ecvrf` 0.0.1, with no audit found
- [ADR 0005](https://github.com/NuvexNetwork/nuvex-docs/blob/main/architecture/adr/0005-fee-model.md) — fee roles; basis points are not chosen
- [ADR 0006](https://github.com/NuvexNetwork/nuvex-docs/blob/main/architecture/adr/0006-callback-model.md) — callback with zero accounts
- [ADR 0007](https://github.com/NuvexNetwork/nuvex-docs/blob/main/architecture/adr/0007-upgrade-model.md) — multisig upgrades; nothing is deployed
